---

Point Ark SDKs and clients at `https://api.apipod.ai/api/v3` and keep the request body identical to the Volcano official API. Poll tasks with the [Ark-compatible query endpoint](/ark-query-video-task).

<Note title="Pinning the omni reference sub-task">Pass `omni_reference_task_type: edit` or `extend` on the bare model to pin the sub-task: the request is routed to the dedicated [seedance-2.5-video-edit](/seedance/2-5-video-edit) / [seedance-2-5-video-extend](/seedance/2-5-video-extend) contracts, where the required source video is validated at submission time and the output ratio (and, for edit, the output duration) automatically follows the source video instead of failing asynchronously. `auto` (default) and `reference` keep the inference behavior.</Note>
