<Note>
MAGI-2 renders native 1080p, 25 fps audio-video clips (finished output is ~0.24s longer than `duration`) and typically takes **13–20 minutes** depending on length. Use `callback_url` or patient polling on `GET /v1/videos/status/{task_id}`. Billing is flat per requested second regardless of aspect ratio or `generate_audio`.
</Note>
